# 947947.com — research, decision and business case

Prepared 8 October 2026. Every external fact links to its source. Figures marked *estimate* are our projections, not measured data.

---

## 1. Decision in one paragraph

Build **947947.com as "the number meaning engine"**: a reference that decodes any number through seven lenses (maths, numerology, angel-number folklore, Chinese, Japanese and Korean/Thai readings, and phone codes and dates), launched with **2,402 statically built number pages**, 12 free tools and 10 sourced guides. Monetise with Google AdSense, a high-intent **personal reading funnel** (psychic and numerology partners pay US$50–100 per new customer), a **B2B business number audit**, labelled sponsorships, a YouTube channel and reader support. The digits 947947 carry no keyword meaning and two 4s that depress resale value in Chinese-speaking markets, so value has to come from what is built on the name.

## 7. Risks and mitigations
- **Scaled-content policy:** every number page carries computed facts unique to that number plus cultural detection; pages limited to searched ranges; guides are sourced. Expand ranges only with demand evidence.
- **AdSense approval** needs the custom domain (not github.io) and enough original content; ads.txt is in place.
- **Form delivery** depends on FormSubmit activation (one-time click in the inbox); then switch to the alias.
- **Folklore content:** clearly framed; no prediction, gambling or medical claims; psychic partners only with disclosure.
- **Build time:** about 3.5–4 minutes for 2,402 pages on GitHub Pages (10-minute limit). Keep total number pages under ~5,000 or move to a prebuilt host before scaling further.
