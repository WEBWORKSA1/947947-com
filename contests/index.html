How to enter

  1. Spot a number. A number plate, receipt total, clock time, house number, price tag or page number that made you stop.
  2. Find its story. Use our number explorer to discover its maths and cultural meanings.
  3. Tell us in up to 300 words. Where you saw it, why it struck you and what you learned. Add a link to a photo if you have one.

Categories

  • Best story: the most surprising, moving or funny encounter with a number.
  • Best find: a number with remarkable maths or cultural meaning, spotted in real life.
  • Best photo: the most striking image of a number in the world (link to your photo).
