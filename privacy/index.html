Who we are

947947.com ("we") is an independent website. Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact page.

What we collect

  • Information you send us: when you use a form (reading request, business audit, contest entry, job application, pledge or contact), we receive the details you type, the page you sent it from and basic campaign information such as the referring site. Forms are delivered to our private inbox by FormSubmit, a form-processing service.
  • Calculator inputs: numbers, names and birth dates typed into our tools are processed in your browser and are not sent to us, unless you submit a form.
  • Cookies and similar technology: Google AdSense uses cookies to serve and measure ads, which may be personalised where you have consented and the law allows. Embedded YouTube videos load only when you press play and use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. We store your light or dark theme choice in your browser.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and provide what you asked for, such as your number blueprint or an audit quote.
  • To run contests, including verifying winners.
  • To send our monthly forecast, only if you opted in. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe.
  • To keep the site secure and understand which pages are useful.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. If you ask about a one-to-one session, we share only the details needed with the numerologist who will contact you.

Advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings or at aboutads.info. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through a certified consent message.

How long we keep it

Enquiries and applications are kept for up to two years, contest records for three years, and newsletter details until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA or California's CCPA), you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your information, or object to its use. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13, and contests are open only to adults. We do not knowingly collect children's information.

Changes

We will post any changes here with a new date.
