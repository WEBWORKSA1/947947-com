## The format

Sri Lanka's country calling code is **+94**. Mobile numbers have ten digits at home, starting with **07**, for example 077 123 4567. Internationally you drop the leading zero and add the country code: **+94 77 123 4567**. That is why every Sri Lankan mobile number in international format begins **+947**.

## Which prefix belongs to which network?

| Prefix | Network |
|---|---|
| 070, 071 | SLT-Mobitel |
| 072, 078 | Hutch (which absorbed Etisalat Sri Lanka in 2018) |
| 074, 076, 077 | Dialog |
| 075 | Airtel, operated by Dialog since the two merged in 2024 |

Numbers can move between networks, so treat the prefix as a guide to where a number started rather than proof of its current network. Sri Lanka has roughly 30 million mobile connections, more than one for every resident.

## How to call or message Sri Lanka from abroad

1. Dial your exit code (00 in most countries, 011 from the US and Canada) or press + on a mobile.
2. Dial **94**.
3. Dial the mobile number **without its leading 0**: 77 123 4567.

For WhatsApp and other messaging apps, save the number as **+94 77 123 4567**. Saving it with the 0 is the most common reason a contact does not show up.

## Why +947 matters to so many families

Sri Lankans working abroad sent home a record US$8.07 billion in 2025, according to the Central Bank of Sri Lanka, and the diaspora is estimated at more than two million people. Tourism also hit a record 2.36 million arrivals in 2025. All of that means a lot of calls and messages to +94 7 numbers.

## Safety tips

- Be wary of missed calls from unknown international numbers; calling back premium-rate numbers can be expensive.
- Never share one-time passcodes, even with someone who claims to be from your bank or network.
- Check money-transfer requests by calling the person on a number you already know.

## +947 versus area code 947

Don't confuse a Sri Lankan number with **area code 947** in Michigan, USA, which is written (947) or +1 947. Read [our guide to area code 947]({{ r }}guides/area-code-947/), or see what else [947]({{ r }}947/) and [947947]({{ r }}947947/) mean.
