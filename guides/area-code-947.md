## Where is area code 947?

Area code 947 serves **Oakland County, Michigan**, plus small parts of Wayne County such as Livonia and Northville. Communities include Troy, Southfield, Farmington Hills, Novi, Pontiac, Rochester Hills, Royal Oak, Birmingham, Bloomfield Hills, Auburn Hills, Waterford, West Bloomfield and Wixom. The area is on Eastern Time.

Oakland County is one of the most affluent large counties in the United States, with about 1.29 million residents in 2025 and a median household income of $97,760, according to the US Census Bureau.

## An overlay of 248

947 is an **overlay**: it covers exactly the same area as area code [248]({{ r }}248/). Area code 248 was split from 810 in 1997; by 2000 it was running short of numbers, so regulators added 947 on top rather than splitting the county again. 947 entered service on **7 September 2002**.

Because two area codes share one area, local calls there need all ten digits, even to a neighbour.

## Can a number be (947) 947-XXXX?

Not yet. In September 2026, numbering records showed no 947 exchange (the middle three digits) assigned within area code 947. The 947 exchange does exist inside **248**: (248) 947 numbers belong to a wireless block in Farmington, Michigan.

## How to call 947 from abroad

Dial your international exit code, then **+1 947** and the seven-digit number, for example +1 947 555 0123. From Canada and the United States, dial 1 947 and the number.

## Do not confuse (947) with +947

A number written **+94 7…** is not in Michigan. +94 is the country code for **Sri Lanka**, and every Sri Lankan mobile number begins +94 7 in international format. If an unexpected call or message shows +947, it comes from a Sri Lankan mobile network. See [how Sri Lankan mobile numbers work]({{ r }}guides/sri-lanka-mobile-numbers/).

## Is a 947 call a scam?

Area code 947 is an ordinary code used by millions of legitimate people and businesses. Scammers, however, can fake ("spoof") any caller ID. If a caller pressures you to pay, asks for codes or claims to be a government agency or bank:

- hang up and call the organisation back on a number you find yourself;
- never share one-time passcodes or card details;
- in the US, report scam calls to the Federal Trade Commission and register on the National Do Not Call Registry.
