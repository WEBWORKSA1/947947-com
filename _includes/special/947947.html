The 947947 report

947947 has no dictionary meaning, which is exactly what makes it interesting. Pull it apart and you find a prime with unusual neighbours, a phone prefix shared by tens of millions of people, a Michigan area code, a Berkeley ZIP region, a tenth-century turning point in China and Japan, and a split verdict from East Asian number lore. The maths: one prime, written twice

947947
947×1001
947×7×11×13

Write any three-digit number twice and you multiply it by 1,001, and 1,001 = 7 × 11 × 13. So 947947 = 7 × 11 × 13 × 947, with exactly 16 divisors. The trick works for every ABCABC number; try it with yours.

The core, 947, is a remarkable prime:

  • It is the 161st prime and a balanced prime: exactly halfway between its neighbouring primes 941 and 953.
  • 941, 947 and 953 form a sexy prime triplet, three primes spaced six apart.
  • It is a Chen prime (947 + 2 = 949 = 13 × 73) and a Gaussian prime (it leaves remainder 3 when divided by 4).
  • It equals the sum of seven consecutive primes: 113 + 127 + 131 + 137 + 139 + 149 + 151.
  • Forty-three straight cuts can slice a pancake into at most 947 pieces (the lazy caterer's sequence).

Phones and postcodes

  • Area code 947, Michigan. An overlay of area code 248 covering Oakland County, in service since 7 September 2002. Oakland County had about 1.29 million residents in 2025 and a median household income of $97,760. A number shaped (947) 947-XXXX does not exist yet: the 947 exchange is unassigned in area code 947, while (248) 947 is a wireless block in Farmington.
  • Sri Lanka, +947. Every Sri Lankan mobile number written internationally starts with +94 7: 070 and 071 for SLT-Mobitel, 072 and 078 for Hutch, 074, 076 and 077 for Dialog, and 075 for Airtel by Dialog. The country has roughly 30 million mobile connections, and workers abroad sent home a record US$8.07 billion in 2025.
  • India, 9470 to 9479. Mobile series allocated to BSNL and MTNL circles from Bihar and Jharkhand to West Bengal, Kolkata, Punjab and Madhya Pradesh (number portability blurs this today). Indian PIN codes beginning with 9 belong to the Army Postal Service.
  • ZIP prefix 947, California. US ZIP codes 947xx serve Berkeley and Albany in the East Bay, routed through Oakland.

What cultures hear

  • Chinese: 9 (jiǔ) sounds like 久, long-lasting; 4 (sì) sounds like 死, death; 7 (qī) is mixed. In Taiwanese internet slang 94 reads as 就是, "exactly", and 9494 as "that's right". We found no established slang meaning for 947 itself.
  • Japanese: 9 and 4 read ku-shi, which spells 櫛, comb; Japan has marked 4 September as Comb Day since 1978. Both 4 (shi) and 9 (ku) are traditionally avoided.
  • Korean: 4 (sa) is avoided, and ages ending in 9 are considered risky years.
  • Thai: 9 (gâo) sounds like stepping forward, a lucky digit.
  • Vedic numerology: 9 is Mars, 4 is Rahu and 7 is Ketu, so 947 joins a fiery planet with both lunar nodes.
  • Western numerology: 947947 adds to 40 and reduces to 4, The Builder.

History and the sky

  • 947 AD, China: the Khitan emperor Taizong of Liao destroyed the Later Jin and entered Kaifeng; on 10 March Liu Zhiyuan founded the Later Han.
  • 947 AD, Japan: the Tenryaku era began in April under Emperor Murakami, and building started on Kyoto's Kitano Shrine.
  • 947 AD, elsewhere: Otto I gave Bavaria to his brother Henry; the historian al-Masʿudi completed his world history; the Chola king Raja Raja I was born around this time.
  • In space: asteroid 947 Monterosa was discovered on 8 February 1921 at Bergedorf, Hamburg, and NGC 947 is a galaxy in Cetus first recorded by John Herschel in 1835.
  • As a colour: #947947 is a muted khaki brown, RGB 148, 121, 71.

The economics of a number

Six-digit .com names were nearly all registered by late 2015, when fewer than 8,000 of the million combinations remained. Patterned names trade higher: reported ABCABC sales run from about US$1,000 to US$20,000, and those containing a 4 cluster between roughly US$1,000 and US$5,500, because buyers in Chinese-speaking markets avoid the digit. 947947.com has been registered since February 2010.

The lesson for any number-named website: the digits carry no keyword, so value has to come from what is built on them. That is why this site turns a number into a useful reference for every number.

A note on the name
