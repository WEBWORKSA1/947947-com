What we do

Every number tells two kinds of story. One is mathematical and exact: 947947 is 7 × 11 × 13 × 947. The other is human: in Mandarin 4 sounds like death, in Japanese 39 says thank you, and millions of people take a second look when a clock reads 11:11. 947947 puts both side by side for thousands of numbers, with tools to explore your own.

How we work it out

  • Maths on number pages is computed automatically when the site is built: factorisation, divisors, bases, Roman numerals and number properties. Anyone can check it.
  • Numerology follows the Pythagorean method by default: digits are added and reduced to 1 to 9, keeping the master numbers 11, 22 and 33. The name calculator also offers Chaldean values.
  • Angel-number readings are interpretive. We build them from the traditional meanings of each digit and its numerology root, and we say clearly that they are folklore.
  • Cultural readings (Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai, Vedic and biblical) come from published sources such as encyclopaedias, language-learning publishers and cultural institutions. Contested or unverified claims are left out or labelled.
  • Phone codes come from Google's open libphonenumber data and the North American Numbering Plan, and country codes from the open mledoze/countries dataset.

Editorial policy

We aim to be useful to believers, sceptics and the simply curious alike. That means we never claim numbers can predict events, we never give medical, legal or financial advice, and we explain the psychology behind seeing repeated numbers alongside the folklore. Our team uses software, including AI tools, to compute facts and draft text; people check cultural claims against the sources cited and correct errors when readers report them.

Corrections: if you spot an error, tell us. We fix verified errors promptly.

How the site is funded

947947 is free. It is funded by display advertising served by Google AdSense, labelled sponsorships, optional paid services such as one-to-one numerology sessions and business number audits, affiliate links that are always disclosed, and reader support. Sponsors and advertisers have no say over editorial content.
