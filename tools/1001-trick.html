Use it as a party trick

Ask a friend to write a three-digit number twice on a calculator. Tell them, with a straight face, that 7 brings luck, so they should divide by 7, and that there will be no remainder. Then 11, then 13. Their original number appears. The full explanation is in our guide, and all 900 results are listed on the ABCABC page.
