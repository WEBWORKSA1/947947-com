How these tools work

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere unless you choose to request a full reading, and even then only the details in that form are sent.

Numerology calculators use the Pythagorean letter values by default (A = 1 through I = 9, then repeating) and keep the master numbers 11, 22 and 33. The name calculator can switch to the older Chaldean values. Treat the interpretations as a tradition for reflection rather than a prediction.
