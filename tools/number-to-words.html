Writing cheques and contracts

In English, hyphenate compound numbers from twenty-one to ninety-nine. US usage normally drops "and" (nine hundred forty-seven); British usage keeps it (nine hundred and forty-seven). Chinese groups digits by ten thousand (万), so 947947 is 九十四万七千九百四十七.
