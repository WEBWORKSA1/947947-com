How the check works

We add every digit and reduce the total to a root number from 1 to 9 (or a master number), then read that root's traditional meaning. We count the digits that matter in Chinese number lore and look for famous codes inside the number, such as 168 (fortune all the way) or 748 (an insult). With your birth date, we compare the number's root with your life path.
