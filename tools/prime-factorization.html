Worked example: 947947

947947 ÷ 7 = 135421; 135421 ÷ 11 = 12311; 12311 ÷ 13 = 947; and 947 is prime because no prime up to its square root (about 30.8) divides it. So 947947 = 7 × 11 × 13 × 947. Every number on this site lists its factors, divisors and more: try 947947 or any number.
