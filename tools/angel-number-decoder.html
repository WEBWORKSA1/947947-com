How a number is decoded

The decoder adds the digits and reduces them to a root number, then reads each digit for its traditional theme. Repeated digits are treated as louder versions of the same message, which is why 111 is read differently from 1. It also checks the sound of each digit in Mandarin, Japanese and Korean, and looks for famous number codes hidden inside, such as 520 (I love you in Chinese slang).
