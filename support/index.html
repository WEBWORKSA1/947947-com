Questions

Is my support tax-deductible?

No. 947947.com is a privately owned website, not a registered charity, so support payments are not charitable donations and are not tax-deductible.

Do supporters get anything?

Our thanks, an optional name on the supporters wall, and early access to new tools. Support never buys influence over our content.

Can I cancel monthly support?

Yes, any time, through the payment provider or by contacting us.

How do you choose what to fund?

Readers' requests and search demand decide which guides, translations and tools come next.
